# Human-calibrated Luna comparison results

September 22, 2026 · [#21](https://github.com/montagovian/basedBench/issues/21)
· [Frozen plan](calibrated-comparison.md) · [Human results](explanation-calibration-results.md)

The bounded comparison is complete: **209 calls, $0.22198508 estimated model
cost, no API or response-validation errors**. The simpler interface worked
reliably in this run. GPT-6 Luna reduced cost but did not establish a quality
improvement over GPT-5.6 Luna with the same interface. Shared human-confirmed
misses and unnecessary objections to ready answers remain. **Do not promote
either challenger to automatic admission or expand #9 from this result.**

The user's GPT-6 Luna suggestion changed the third condition to a model-version
comparison. It did not test a more expensive capacity tier. The official
[GPT-6 Luna model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna)
lists standard input/output prices of $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens, versus
$0.20/$1.20 for [GPT-5.6 Luna](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-luna).
Availability was checked with this account before inference. Exact prices,
settings, code, prompts, schemas and requests were frozen before each stage.

## What the human evidence says to fix

- **Undecoded wordplay still passes.** All three conditions accept the quiz-name
  answer that the human rejected for failing to explain the puns. Both simpler
  conditions do catch missing Latin decoding in another human repair case.
- **Vague placeholders still pass.** All three accept the closet explanation's
  vague figure/object, despite the human request to identify the visible face.
- **Necessary context can be found, inconsistently.** All three catch the
  missing Resident Evil 4 connection. Simpler 5.6 catches the XM8 contrast
  defect; simpler 6 passes it, contrary to the human repair judgment.
- **Some demands are unnecessary.** All three fail Sonic/Minecraft and domino
  originals that the human found adequate, demanding extra identity or layout
  detail. Requiring exhaustive description would tighten the task incorrectly.
- **Source uncertainty should remain distinct.** Luna 6 holds the disputed
  Israel/fashion and Mystique readings while finding the answers adequate.
  The human notes support keeping those interpretations unresolved. The dog
  and algae answers remain human-ready even when source counts cause holds.

These conclusions use the actual saved human labels and qualifying notes.
Earlier assistant inspection flags remain hypotheses; they are not additional
negative labels or a reason to reject simple memes.

## Fresh cases and all five disagreements

Before fresh outputs, 30 identities were selected by fixed hash from the 48
remaining eligible cached candidates. Broader local exposure/family retrieval
then held **14**, leaving **16** for paired calls. Held cases were not replaced.
All 30 have screening records. Matches use exact/full/trimmed images, aspect
crops and saved semantic vectors; possible links are conservative exposure
holds, not 14 confirmed duplicate judgments. The expanded crop pass also
recovers both duplicate pairs found during human calibration.

The fresh stage uses the existing 5.6 checker and simpler Luna 6, with unchanged
recipes and 32 calls. It uses cached original answers, not new generation or a
complete chronological admission run. Family separation is limited to the
recorded retrieval coverage. No new human labels were collected, so the
following are **routing outcomes, not accuracy or usable-item counts**:

| Condition | Combined pass | Answer fail | Evidence/uncertainty hold |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Existing 5.6 checker | 11 | 4 | 1 |
| Simpler 6 checker | 10 | 4 | 2 |

Simpler 6 separately finds 12 answers adequate and four defective; two of those
12 adequate answers remain source holds. Conditions disagree on five combined
outcomes. Assistant inspection examined each image, answer and supplied comment
set after the run. These findings are unblinded development observations, not
human gold; the local casebook conceals condition identities for later review.

| Case | Disagreement and inspection finding |
| --- | --- |
| `1tyza96`, Cameron Diaz pun | Baseline fails; Luna 6 passes. The answer already decodes the sound-alike phrase. Demanding that it also repeat the visible colonoscopy setup appears unnecessarily strict. |
| `1u9gb85`, Kevin Rose/Digg | Baseline passes; Luna 6 holds support while passing adequacy. Two comments explain the failed competition; the third gives biographical context. This is a support-count question, not demonstrated answer failure. |
| `1u8dptp`, Fahrenheit | Baseline passes; Luna 6 fails. Luna 6 imports a Celsius freezing/boiling rebuttal from comments and calls it the image's missing payoff. The actual image contains two pro-Fahrenheit statements. This is a concrete checker error observed by the assistant. |
| `1u2ywbz`, Canadians/cans | Baseline fails; Luna 6 passes adequacy but holds support. The answer decodes the pun; repeating that the pictured cans are literal cans appears unnecessary. Only two comments explain the connection; the third is a riff. |
| `1tyxtkk`, ladder chain | Baseline holds support; Luna 6 additionally fails adequacy. The answer shifts the final complaint and blame away from the pictured ladder taker. That is a plausible role-attribution defect; both conditions still lack substantive consensus support. |

No new repairs were attempted. The plan allowed up to ten, not a mandatory
quota; these findings did not justify enlarging the run or inventing fresh
repair-success labels. Cost per human-usable unique fresh item is **unavailable**
because the fresh outcomes lack human adjudication. Passing counts cannot fill
that denominator.

At the same interface, Luna 6 costs about **38% less** than 5.6 in development.
Prices and token usage both affect this observed difference. Conservative
accounting totals **$0.23731625**, below the $3 development, $6 fresh and $10
overall ceilings. No retries, reserve calls, pending usage or allowance
violations remain. One animated input hold is retained separately from the
zero technical-error count. Costs are usage-based estimates, not invoices.

Both runs replay without API access and preserve every artifact byte. The
pre-run preservation inventory covers 11,326 older files, including frozen
experiments, the database, completed human feedback and unrelated working edits.
All **468 tests pass** (466 without socket binding and two local HTTP tests
with loopback access). Final verification records 496 unchanged files across
the two zero-call replays and all 11,326 protected older files unchanged.
Raw examples, images,
feedback and calls remain under ignored `data/`; public documentation contains
only the bounded findings.

The next useful experiment should target **required decoding and visible
referents**, while retaining these human-ready controls. Require a concrete
missing connection before failing an answer; test that any demanded detail
actually belongs to the image's joke rather than a comment's alternative.
Keep source-support decisions separate. Use a small discriminating set before
another broad sample; compare a genuinely higher-capacity condition only under
a separately frozen price/call plan. This run leaves that capacity question open.

The 50 human reviews supplied enough direction for this next iteration; a
larger general annotation round is not the immediate bottleneck. A few fresh
borderline judgments could later resolve the new hypotheses. Broader duplicate
retrieval should retain the two newly found families as controls before any
chronological expansion. Keep #9 pending an explicit later decision, with
suitability, source rules and all prior admission outcomes unchanged.
