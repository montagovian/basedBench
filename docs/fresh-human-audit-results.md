above unchanged.

from the written caption without identifying a face or verifying the underlying
news event. The saved source context contains “Swifty Peter here” in comment

development hypothesis, not an established explanation of the model's behavior.
A useful correction must anchor the subject and the awkward wedding-invitation
question to the image; source narrators must not become image subjects.

**Philosophers needs the specific connection, not just a generic description.**
The human says the answer misses the depicted individuals and the implication
that their conduct/reputation explains the villain comparison, generalized to
philosophers. Saved comments `osf7lba` and `osf88i7` discuss Wittgenstein and
Schopenhauer. Those attributions and any biographical claims need verification
before drafting a correction; this report does not establish them from faces
or treat comments as verified biography. This case does not create a universal
requirement to enumerate every name or detail in otherwise adequate answers.

**Bowsette has a repair label and unresolved interpretation.** Comments
`osdr7r9` and `osdsd1b` discuss a sexual implication of controlling Bowsette's
body, while `osdqohb` and `ose0hj1` discuss crown removal turning her back into
Bowser. The human explicitly doubts consistent ground truth. Do not turn this
into a confidently specified single gold rewrite, or treat the sexual reading
as an automatic suitability rejection.

**Death Note stays ready, with its qualification.** The explicit uncertainty
